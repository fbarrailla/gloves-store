Description

The Apex 001 is built for the keeper who comes off the line. A negative seam wraps a four-millimetre contact-latex palm, finished by hand at our workshop in Bandung. The sodium yellow on the index and middle fingers travels under floodlights; the ink backhand doesn't.

Tested by R.S., a Persib reserve keeper, across an eleven-week prototyping cycle. Forty-two pairs of Apex were made for Drop 07 — twelve in this colorway.

Shipping & returns

Shipping — Ships from Bandung, Indonesia. Domestic 3–5 working days, international 7–10. Tracked on every order. Free over IDR 1.5M.

Returns — 14 days for unworn pairs in original packaging. Worn pairs are eligible for our re-palm program at 50% of retail.

Care

Wash the palm in cool water — no soap, no bleach — after each match. Lay flat to dry, palm up, away from direct sun. Forty-eight hours later your gloves are ready again. The contact latex is alive; treat it kindly.

Why Apex 001

01

4 mm contact latex

Single-source German latex, graded for contact at the highest level. We sit with each batch for a week before cutting.

02

Tight 2.5 mm wrap brings the palm closer to the ball — more feedback, more spin, less material between you and the shot.
